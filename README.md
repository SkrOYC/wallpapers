# Wallpaper Collection

A curated collection of high-resolution desktop wallpapers featuring nature, landscapes, minimal architecture, abstract artwork, and deep space photography.

---

## Highlights & Sources

- **Curated Landscapes & Nature:** Classic wallpapers curated across diverse environments, mountains, forests, and oceans.
- **Brave NTP Artwork:** New Tab Page background sets showcasing works by independent photographers and visual artists.
- **Space & Astronomy:** High-resolution imagery from NASA and the Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI / James Webb & Hubble).
- **Format Variety:** High-quality JPG, PNG, and modern WebP formats suitable for 1080p, 1440p, 4K, and ultrawide displays.

---

## Repository Structure

Wallpapers are stored at the root of the repository:
- Curated desktop wallpapers named with descriptive slugs (`<descriptive-slug>.jpg`).
- Artist-named wallpapers (`<artist-name>.jpg` / `.webp`) from community and browser artwork releases.
- Space imagery (`STScI-*.png`) from public space telescope archives.
